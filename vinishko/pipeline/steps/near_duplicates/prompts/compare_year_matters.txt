When an ELEMENT has a non-empty vintage field in CARD_JSON, the vintage is a required product-identity attribute. A clearly printed vintage on QUERY must exactly match that ELEMENT's vintage. A different readable vintage is a hard conflict: an adjacent vintage of the same line is a different SKU. A missing, hidden, or unreadable vintage on QUERY is unknown rather than a conflict, but it cannot outrank another candidate whose exact vintage match is confirmed.
